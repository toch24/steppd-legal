Privacy Policy

Effective October 3, 2026

StepSocial ("StepSocial," "we," "us") is an iOS app operated by an individual developer in the United States. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the StepSocial app and related services (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have. If you have questions, email tomchong.dev@gmail.com.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not show ads, and we do not share your phone number or SMS consent with anyone for marketing.

1. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Phone number. You sign up and log in with your mobile number. We use it to text you a one-time verification code. Your number is never shown on your profile.
  • Profile information. Your username, an optional display name, an optional profile photo, and whether your account is private.
  • Posts. Photos you choose to post, with your step count drawn onto the image, plus the step count, your streak count, and the date of the post.
  • Social activity. Friend requests you send, accept, or decline; your friends list; and reactions you leave on posts.

Information from your device

  • Step count (Apple Health). With your permission, the app reads your daily step count from Apple Health on your device. Only the step count attached to a post you publish is sent to our servers. We do not upload any other Health data.
  • Camera and photo library. With your permission, the app opens the camera or photo library so you can pick a photo. We only receive photos you choose to post or use as your profile photo. The app re-encodes photos before upload, which removes embedded metadata such as location.
  • Technical data. Our hosting provider automatically records basic request information, such as IP address, timestamps, and app version, to operate and secure the Service.

We do not collect your location, contacts, or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no third-party analytics or advertising software.

2. How we use information

  • To create and secure your account, including sending verification codes by SMS.
  • To show your profile and posts and to run features such as friends, reactions, streaks, search, and notifications.
  • To keep the Service safe, prevent abuse and fraud, and fix problems.
  • To respond to your requests and contact you about your account.
  • To comply with law and enforce our Terms and Conditions.

We do not use Apple Health data for advertising or data mining, and we never sell it or give it to advertisers or data brokers.

3. SMS messaging

When you enter your phone number and tap "Text me a code," you ask us to send you a text message with a one-time verification code. We send verification codes only. We do not send marketing or promotional texts. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers that deliver our messages.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other users. Any signed-in user can find your username, display name, and profile photo. If your account is public, any signed-in user can see your posts. If your account is private, only friends you approve can see your posts. Your phone number is never shown to other users.
  • Service providers. We use a small number of companies to run the Service, and only for that purpose: Supabase (database, file storage, and sign-in hosting) and Twilio (delivering SMS verification codes). They handle your information on our behalf under their own privacy and security commitments.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or legal process, or if needed to protect the rights, property, or safety of StepSocial, our users, or others.
  • Transfers. If StepSocial is sold or merged, your information may move to the new owner, who will remain bound by this policy.

5. Data retention

We keep your information for as long as your account exists. When your account is deleted, we delete your profile, posts, photos, friendships, reactions, and notifications within 30 days, except where the law requires us to keep something longer. Backups that contain deleted data are overwritten on a regular schedule.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Edit or remove content. You can change your profile and make your account private in the app's settings.
  • Permissions. You can turn off Health, camera, photo, or notification access at any time in iOS Settings.
  • Access, correction, and deletion. To get a copy of your data, correct it, or delete your account, email tomchong.dev@gmail.com from your account's phone number or include it in your message. We respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example California, or the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom), you may have additional rights, such as to know what we collect, to object to or restrict processing, and to data portability. We honor these rights for all users and will not treat you differently for using them. Where the GDPR applies, we process your information to provide the Service you asked for, for our legitimate interest in keeping it secure, and with your consent for Health, camera, and photo access. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, and database access rules limit each account to the data it is allowed to see. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

StepSocial is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

9. International users

StepSocial is run from the United States. Your information may be stored and processed in the United States and other countries where our service providers operate.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here and update the effective date. If the changes are significant, we will also tell you in the app.
